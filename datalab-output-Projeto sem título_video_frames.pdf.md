## Got questions?

### Can the FMD be implemented without collective bargaining (NAO)?

Yes. The FMD does not need to be negotiated as part of a NAO to exist within a company.

The Ministry of Ecological Transition clarifies that negotiating the implementation of the FMD is not mandatory, even though mobility topics must be addressed in NAO processes for certain companies with 50 or more employees and employee representatives.

In practice, the FMD can therefore be implemented unilaterally by the employer, enabling a quick and positive rollout of the scheme.

### Can the FMD be implemented unilaterally?

Yes. In the absence of a collective agreement, the employer can define the amount and terms of FMD coverage independently, after consulting the works council (CSE) where one exists.

This flexibility allows companies to move forward without adding to their social calendar, while keeping the implementation framework secure.
